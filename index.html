Lizard Lizard Lizard

The ultimate one-button lizard clicker. Click the lizard, hear the lizard, beat your high score.

About the Game

Lizard Lizard Lizard is a free browser-based clicker game featuring Tom the Lizard. Every click makes a sound and bumps your counter. Your high score is saved locally, so you can keep trying to beat your personal best.

How to Play

Click the big green button as many times as you can. Each click plays a lizard sound and adds to your counter. Beat your previous record and the high score updates automatically. Use the reset buttons to start over.

Features

Instant audio feedback, persistent high score tracking, mobile-friendly touch support, and an animated starfield background. No ads, no sign-up, no tracking — just lizards.
